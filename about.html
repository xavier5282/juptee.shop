The person behind JUPTEE

Creative roots. Global outlook.

Joyonto Rozario is a multidisciplinary UI/UX designer, creative technologist, and entrepreneur from Bangladesh. Born in Gulshan, Dhaka, and raised in the quieter surroundings of Savar, his story was shaped by two very different sides of the city.

Driven by a passion for art, design, and technology, Joyonto later moved to Farmgate, stepping into the fast pace of central Dhaka. He began his career as a freelance designer on international platforms before building ventures of his own.

Today, as founder of JUPTEE and J7 Connect Ltd., Joyonto leads creative direction across custom apparel, digital platforms, and technology solutions—building global-minded brands from Bangladesh.
